Option 2: delete it in the app

  1. Open the Doggy Ji app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom menu.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, type DELETE in the box, then tap Delete.

Option 3: ask us to delete it

Can't sign in anywhere? Send us a request. We will check that it comes from the account's owner (we may ask you to reply from the same email address), then delete the account. We reply within 30 days.

What happens to your data

DeletedKept, without your name
  • Your profile and login
  • Your pets and their photos
  • Pet Match listings and likes
  • Chats, messages and the Doggy Ji chat
  • Friends and notifications
  • Blood donor registrations
  • Bookings and reviews you wrote
  • Service provider profile and documents
  • Orders: sales records held by our store and for tax, unlinked from your app account

  • Reports you filed: kept for moderation, without naming you

Chat images are removed automatically after 24 hours in any case. For more detail see our privacy policy.
