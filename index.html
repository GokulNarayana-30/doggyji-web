How the App Works in Practice

1

Discover & Shop

Parent opens app. Reorders favorite air-dried treats, reviews daily feeding goals, or taps "Pet Services".

2

Find Verified Pro

Browses local walkers or sitters, reviews ratings, badges (Verified Pro), background checks, and past reviews.

3

Instant 2-Tap Booking

Selects saved pet, picks time slot, adds custom leash/feeding instructions, and completes transparent booking.

4

Live Updates & Coins

Receives photo updates during the walk via chat, leaves a review, and earns Paw Points for free treats.

Become a Partner

3

Safety Quiz Gate

Passes the 5-question Pet Safety & First Aid test (80% passing gate) ensuring certified quality care standards.

4

Live Dashboard

Toggles Online/Offline status, accepts client bookings, coordinates walks, and receives weekly direct payouts.
